About

Co-Parenting Planner — Premium Edition v2.0.0 A private, offline-ready co-parenting organizer. All your data lives only in this browser via LocalStorage — nothing is sent to any server.

What's new in v2.0 Custody rotation engine (2-2-3, 7-7, 2-2-5-5, alternating weekends), Settle Up button, dark mode, active parent filter, enhanced legal log with timestamps, printable emergency cards, and more.

To back up your data, use "Export Backup" regularly. Clearing your browser's storage will erase all data permanently.
